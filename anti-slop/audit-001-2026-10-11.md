# Copywriting audit 001 (2026-10-11)

Mode: **After** (audit). Scope: prose across the 18 English pages and 18 Indonesian pages under `docs/`, plus `README.md`. Nothing was changed by this audit.

## Method

1. Mechanical scan of every page with 16 English pattern groups and 8 Indonesian ones (empty AI vocabulary, significance inflation, weasel attribution, signposting, chatbot closers, fake-candid openers, persuasive authority tropes, filler phrases, stacked hedging, generic positive conclusions, inanimate-subject/human-verb, false ranges, rule-of-three, negative parallelism, tailing negations, staccato runs, empty social proof, fabricated statistics).
2. Hard-gate greps: em dash, en dash, double hyphen, buzzword list, generic CTA list, fabricated-claim patterns.
3. Link check across all 36 pages.
4. Read-through of the landing page, Sync, Credentials, Database, Settings, Insights, Tasks, and Local Dev in full.

## Two near-misses, both false positives

1. `docs/database.html:147` — "Opens a file on disk. No server, no credentials."
   The tailing negation reads as a copy tell in isolation. In context it is a row in the SQLite column of an engine comparison table, stating a technical fact that contrasts with the MySQL and PostgreSQL rows above it. Keep.

2. `docs/settings.html:469` — "Used by sites that are not pinned to a version of their own."
   Matched the empty-social-proof pattern. Here "used by" is literal: it describes which sites the default PHP version applies to. Keep.

## Why the copy passes

- **Specifics, not adjectives.** "AES-256-GCM", "Argon2", "Turso", "dnsmasq", "APFS", "launchd", "5 minutes", "30 days". The pages name the actual mechanism instead of describing it as secure or powerful.
- **Limits stated plainly.** Sync says it "does not keep history, so a deletion on one machine becomes a deletion on the other". Local Dev says "macOS only". Install says "Apple Silicon only for now. Intel builds are not published." That is the opposite of the pattern the rules target.
- **The reader is assumed technical.** "which is why the app stays fast and keeps working with no network" explains a consequence rather than making a claim.
- **Actor present.** "Badami keeps a local database", "Badami installs and manages", "you paste the key". No actorless passive.

## Notes for the next audit

- **R-14 (identical feature cards).** The 12 module entries on the landing page share identical markup, size, and layout. Every entry links to a real doc page and carries its own two-line description, so the uniformity is a deliberate index rather than decoration. Worth revisiting only if the module list grows past what one screen can hold.
- **R-31 (reason per decision).** No `DESIGN.md` exists. The docs' visual decisions (palette, radius scale, terminal chrome) are recorded in commit messages rather than a design file. A `DESIGN.md` would give the next person one place to read them.
